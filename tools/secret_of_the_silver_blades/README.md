# secret_of_the_silver_blades

Scripts for Secret of the Silver Blades: staging, loading, training and comparing its saves.

| file | purpose |
|---|---|
| `ssbarm16fight.py` | The earlier version of `ssbarm16quick.py`: same teleport and step onto 15,12, but the fight is driven with `melee_turn` on a 90 second watch and a 300 second budget. Claims a pool slot; writes under the tool's scratch directory. #334 (The session driver cannot fight in Curse or Silver Blades, and says the party is not in a fight while it is standing on the combat floor). |
| `ssbarm16quick.py` | Teleports the position triple to Silver Blades 15,11, steps onto 15,12 to run `ECL10` arm 16, and drives the assassin fight with the game's `QUICK` command. The later version of `ssbarm16fight.py`. Claims a pool slot; writes under the tool's scratch directory. #334 (The session driver cannot fight in Curse or Silver Blades, and says the party is not in a fight while it is standing on the combat floor). |
| `ssbarm2route.py` | Static, no emulator: lists `ECL10`'s arm 2 (the 14,11 shop) statements with strings shown as lengths, then prints `GEO10`'s attribute grid and searches for a route to 15,12 that never enters 14,11. Takes `--from`, `--to` and `--also` address ranges and reads the Silver Blades disks and the walked engine-resave specimen. #334 (The session driver cannot fight in Curse or Silver Blades, and says the party is not in a fight while it is standing on the combat floor). |
| `ssbdisk.py` | Builds a *Secret of the Silver Blades* C64 save disk from a DOS save folder — the third of three, after `tools/dos/dosdisk.py` and `tools/curse_of_the_azure_bonds/cursedisk.py`, because this title's container is one 7424-byte `SAVEDBASH` at `$4B00` with three header rows that are neither of the others'. `--sheet` prints the DOS party the way the C64 `VIEW` screen lays it out, so the two can be read against each other; `--check-areas` re-derives the twenty-two area rows off the player's own disks and diffs them against `goldbox/areas.py`. Reaches past `goldbox.dos_codec.CONVERTS` in its own process only, and says so. |
| `ssbedit.py` | Edits a *Secret of the Silver Blades* save in Wish and then reads the edits off the game's own screens, which is `#33 (One Silver Blades session, for the whole editor path)` step 5. `stage` copies an engine-written save disk and changes a named character's name, gold and strength **through `EditorBinding`** -- the program's own `_flush`, `_write_back` and `editor.files.save_disk`, not a hand-written record poke -- and reports what each field held before and after together with the `+$C00` name table, which those three fields were chosen to interrogate. `run` claims a pooled VICE slot, stages the six sides through `ssbwarp.py`, boots through the cracker intro, loads the party, and photographs the party-formation panel and the character sheet, writing one JSON line per event as it goes so a run that dies halfway still says how far it got. It also carries the two readers `#435 (A rename in Wish leaves the C64 name table holding the old name on Curse and Silver Blades, and nobody knows what reads it)` needs: `name_table` lifts the sixteen-bytes-per-entry table out of a save's payload, and `table_index` asks the container which entry belongs to a slot, since Silver Blades indexes it in marching order and Curse in slot order. |
| `ssbloadescape.py` | The `ssb15` boot with a single Escape sent once `ECL10` starts loading, to test whether Escape aborts the KERNAL load; counterpart of `ssbloadnoescape.py`. Claims a pool slot; writes under the tool's scratch directory. #334 (The session driver cannot fight in Curse or Silver Blades, and says the party is not in a fight while it is standing on the combat floor). |
| `ssbloadnoescape.py` | The `ssb14` boot with no Escape ever sent, polling slot 8's KERNAL end address and `ECL10`'s tail to see whether the script then loads whole; counterpart of `ssbloadescape.py`. Claims a pool slot; writes under the tool's scratch directory. #334 (The session driver cannot fight in Curse or Silver Blades, and says the party is not in a fight while it is standing on the combat floor). |
| `ssbloadwatch.py` | Arms a non-stopping store checkpoint over `$9AC8`-`$9B64` at the Silver Blades party menu, before `LOAD SAVED GAME`, and dumps `$8000`-`$9FFF` plus the loader tables at the menu, after the load and at the world bar, to find what writes the tail of `ECL10`. Claims a pool slot and boots the game with the walked engine-resave specimen from `$WISH_SPECIMENS`; writes under the tool's scratch directory. #334 (The session driver cannot fight in Curse or Silver Blades, and says the party is not in a fight while it is standing on the combat floor). |
| `ssbresavewalk.py` | Drives a converted Secret of the Silver Blades C64 save disk in VICE through `ssbwarp.SSBSession`: loads the party, arrives, reads one sheet by pressing VIEW and reading the raw screen, walks one square and lets the engine's own ENCAMP > SAVE resave, keeping screenshots, a JSON-lines log, `summary.json` and the resaved disk. The `DosToC64` Silver Blades walk of #52 (File ▸ Import and File ▸ Export for every direction the library supports), from before `tools/convert/convertrun.py` had a Silver Blades driver. Claims one pool slot; `--disks`, `--produced` and `--out` as arguments. |
| `ssbreturnprobe.py` | Repeats the `ssbstep1512.py` setup, then sends one KERNAL Return once the message is on screen and reads the interpreter state before and after, driving a fight if the mode byte reaches combat. Claims a pool slot; writes under the tool's scratch directory. #334 (The session driver cannot fight in Curse or Silver Blades, and says the party is not in a fight while it is standing on the combat floor). |
| `ssbrevalidate.py` | Re-runs the walk to Silver Blades 15,11 then 15,12 after the `enter_world` Escape fix and checks `in_combat()`, driving a fight with `Session.fight()` if one starts. Claims a pool slot; writes under the tool's scratch directory. #334 (The session driver cannot fight in Curse or Silver Blades, and says the party is not in a fight while it is standing on the combat floor), and #568 (cursewarp.py and ssbwarp.py can abort a mid-load ECL script by sending Escape to a screen that is merely slow, not stuck). |
| `ssbrun.py` | A driven Silver Blades session on a pooled VICE instance, with a save disk this project built staged as `SIDE0` and the session left serving for `tools/c64/porcmd`. The boot itself is `ssbwarp.py`'s — the cracker intro, the twenty-five seconds of silence, the fastloader prompt arriving after the intro, `INSERT SIDE A` in letters — so this file adds only the save disk and the serve. |
| `ssbsavediff.py` | Diffs two `SAVEDBASH` payloads region by region and says whose each differing byte is, so the engine's own `ENCAMP > SAVE` can be used as the oracle a conversion is checked against. `tools/curse_of_the_azure_bonds/cursesavediff.py` is Curse's and the two region tables are deliberately not the same: five header rows change hands between the titles. Non-zero exit while any run lands in a region a conversion writes. |
| `ssbstage.py` | Copies a *Secret of the Silver Blades* save and sets `$4C2D` (`SAVEDBASH`'s load address `$4B00` plus file offset `$12D`) to 1 -- the byte `#334 (The session driver cannot fight in Curse or Silver Blades, and says the party is not in a fight while it is standing on the combat floor)`'s own disassembly names as the wandering-encounter roll's fight-or-compliment gate, off by default on every specimen driven so far. Nothing else in the save is touched, and the input is opened read only. |
| `ssbstep1512.py` | Stages the party at Silver Blades 15,11, takes one step onto 15,12 and reads eleven addresses at +0, +5, +15, +30 and +60 seconds, with a watchpoint on `$4C05`. Claims a pool slot; writes readings and dumps under the tool's scratch directory. #334 (The session driver cannot fight in Curse or Silver Blades, and says the party is not in a fight while it is standing on the combat floor). |
| `ssbtailprobe.py` | Reads block 28 of `ECL10` (`$9AC8`-`$9B64`) at the Silver Blades world bar, at 15,11 and at 15,12, with a store checkpoint armed between the first two readings. Claims a pool slot; writes under the tool's scratch directory. #334 (The session driver cannot fight in Curse or Silver Blades, and says the party is not in a fight while it is standing on the combat floor). |
| `ssbtrain.py` | Stages a *Secret of the Silver Blades* save disk with experience, platinum, constitution, race, the class bits, the dual-class pair, the rolled hit points and any per-class level written into named character slots, boots it on a pooled VICE instance through `ssbrun.py`, and diffs a before/after record pair -- checking the five stored saving throws against `goldbox.levels.saving_throws` first, and then whatever `goldbox.levelup.plan` can reproduce for the title. `tools/curse_of_the_azure_bonds/cursetrain.py` for the third title, and the lever is the same byte: Silver Blades' party-menu builder is Curse's `$12AF` moved to `GEN $0991`, still `LDY $7EA8 / BNE / AND #$F7`, so poking `$7EA8` to 127 opens the training hall wherever the party stands. A slot is found by the name inside the record rather than by the name table at `+$C00`, which runs in marching order in this title. Written for `#344 (A converted Silver Blades dwarf, gnome or halfling keeps DOS's saving throws, because that title's racial bonus has never been watched in the game)`. |
| `ssbtrainerinputs.py` | Reads off the player's disks the three trainer inputs *Secret of the Silver Blades*' entry in `goldbox/levels.py` still lacks -- `hp_bonus_by_score`, `thief_skills` with `thief_skill_race`, and `wisdom_bonus_level` -- prints them, and checks each against the same table in Curse's files and against a character the game itself wrote, for `#89 (Silver Blades' trainer grants spells from a table, and goldbox/levelup.py offers them from a menu)`. Nothing is transcribed into the repository, and `--rows` prints what each race code gets from the thief's racial row, which the game reads one row too far. |
| `ssbtwins.py` | Converts the six DOS characters SSI shipped and diffs each against the C64 record SSI shipped for the same character, field by field. A consistency check rather than proof — neither side has a chain of custody — but it is what found the ranger arriving as a paladin and the lower-case name, before either was watched in the game. |
| `ssbwarp.py` | Fast-travels a running *Secret of the Silver Blades* party on a pooled slot, to turn `goldbox.areas.AREAS_SILVER_BLADES`' rows into measurements: it stages the six sides, boots, loads a party, and hands each target to `automap.actions.FastTravel` -- the code a player clicking Fast Travel runs -- then reads the cache slot, the disk byte, the live square and the 1024 bytes at `$0400` back. `--to` takes a comma-separated **chain**, driven in one boot, because a boot costs five minutes and a hop thirty seconds. `--spoil-from N` writes a square no row carries in front of a hop, so a table square read back afterwards can only be the arriving script's own doing. It carries five things about driving this rip that `tools/c64/session.py` does not know: a **cracker intro** stands in front of the game and answers to none of its keys; the **fastloader prompt comes after that intro**, not before it, so a driver that waits for it up front waits forever; the loader **letters its sides**, `INSERT SIDE A` through `F` for sides 1 to 6; the party never reaches a command bar, so a trip leaves from the first moment the machine idles in `DUNGEON`'s key-wait loop or the `LIBRARY $4101` fetcher; and a landing is judged by the cache slot and the resident map, never by the program counter -- an arrival on an encounter bar idles above `$6900` in neither key window. |
