# Scratch files, and what counts as a tool

* Game data comes from `gamedisks.yaml`, the registry, and is read-only.
* Anything worth keeping is committed.
* Everything else is written under the temp directory (`tools/scratch.py`), may vanish at any time, and its disappearance never needs a note.
